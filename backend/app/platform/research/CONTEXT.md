# Research ownership

This package owns capability-neutral research structure under a project:
studies, questions, hypotheses, dataset links, experiments, comparisons, and
findings. A project may have no studies or several studies.

Generic runs and shared artifacts may reference experiments and studies, but
module-specific execution state remains in its capability module. Public
presentation is owned by `platform/publication`; research records are private
unless copied into an explicit immutable publication snapshot.
